# The AI Surfer Clone Kit

A friendly, step-by-step guide to your own voice clone and video clone. It walks a business
owner from zero to a clone that looks and sounds like them, plus four starter videos, without
any tech skills.

## What it does

The kit runs in six steps, with a branded card at each one:

1. **Your Brain:** first a quick welcome: connect the Clone Kit, pick where your "brain" folder
   lives (we suggest your cloud drive) and sign in to HeyGen and ElevenLabs. Then the kit builds
   your brain from what you already have: your website, documents, videos or podcasts, what
   your own AI knows about you, and your brand files. Then one short voice interview (10
   questions at most) fills the gaps and asks about your why and the work you love. All saved as
   simple notes in your folder. At the end you can do the full 90/10 as an optional bonus, now or
   any time.
2. **Your Voice:** a quick preview clone, then a professional voice clone in your own
   ElevenLabs account, from recordings you have or a guided reading.
3. **Your Clone:** a check of your video footage before anything is paid, then a video twin
   in your own HeyGen account, with your consent.
4. **Test & Tune:** a 20-second test video in your voice and words, adjusted until it sounds
   like you.
5. **Starters:** a welcome video, a short social clip, a client explainer and a thank-you
   follow-up.
6. **Done:** your clone is ready to make new videos any time.

The kit never spends HeyGen or ElevenLabs credits without your yes to the exact item and its
estimated cost, and it checks what you already have first so nothing is paid for twice. It
never asks for passwords or keys: you sign in on each company's own page.

## How to use it

1. Install the plugin in the Claude desktop app.
2. Add the Clone Kit connection to your Claude account: **Customize → Connectors → + Add → Add
   custom connector**, name it **Clone Kit**, address `https://clonekit.theaisurfershowroom.com/mcp`,
   then **Continue** and type the access password from your welcome email. (The kit walks you
   through this too.) When a card sends a message (like at the end of the interview), Claude
   puts it in your message box: press Enter.
3. Open the app in **Code** mode (the interview card can use your microphone there) on any
   folder.
4. Type `/ai-surfer-clone-kit:start`. The kit takes it from there and remembers where you left off.

You need a paid Claude plan and your own HeyGen and ElevenLabs accounts (a paid HeyGen plan
for a video twin; ElevenLabs Creator or higher for a professional voice clone). Works on a Mac
today. Obsidian is optional, for reading your notes.

## What data goes where

- **Your brain folder** (your computer or your cloud drive): your notes, the documents you drop
  in, the words from your videos, your scripts and clone settings. This is the only copy of
  your brain, apart from the North Star copy below. Documents you add stay here.
- **The AI Surfer's server** (`clonekit.theaisurfershowroom.com`): your interview answers, and
  the audio of spoken answers so it can be turned into text. The interview card's spoken
  questions also come from this server.
- **Your Clone Kit account** (on The AI Surfer's server): the kit keeps a copy of your North
  Star results (your why, your 10%, and your full 90/10 if you do it), so they're safe if
  anything happens to your computer. Your other brain notes and files stay only on your
  computer. To have this copy deleted, email hello@theaisurfer.com.
- **Videos and podcasts you pick:** only the video or audio files you choose are sent to
  The AI Surfer's server. It writes out the words, sends them back to your brain folder, and
  does not keep your files.
- **Your website:** Claude reads its public pages if you give the address.
- **Your own HeyGen account:** your video footage, consent clip, video twin and videos, using
  your plan's credits.
- **Your own ElevenLabs account:** your voice recordings, voice clone and voice clips, using
  your plan's credits.

The plugin itself holds no keys, passwords or personal data. It is only a small starter: the
kit's step-by-step guide is delivered by the Clone Kit connection once you've signed in, so the
kit always runs the latest version and needs that connection to work.

## Bring your brain from Claude (optional)

If you've used Claude for a while, open a regular Claude chat and type
`/ai-surfer-clone-kit:export-brain`. Claude gathers what it already knows about you (its memory and
your past chats) into one file. Save it into your brain's Inbox folder and the kit sorts it in. It
leaves out private details a clone never needs. The file stays with you.
